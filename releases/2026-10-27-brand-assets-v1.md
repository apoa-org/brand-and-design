# APOA Brand Assets V1 · 2026-10-27

**Status:** Approved / public brand download package

## Public entry points

- [APOA Brand Manual](https://apoa-org.github.io/brand-and-design/)
- [Asset Download Centre](https://apoa-org.github.io/brand-and-design/downloads/)

## Included

- **Presentation Template V1** — [editable 12-slide PowerPoint](../downloads/APOA-Presentation-Template-V1.pptx), corrected by the original designer, file bytes preserved.
- **Logo System V1** — 9 distinct approved layouts, each in SVG (vector) and PNG (transparent), named `APOA-01-Primary` through `APOA-09-Horizontal`.
- **Previews** — original presentation cover screenshot and corresponding SVG preview for each Logo in the web gallery.
- **Guidelines** — Brand Manual V1, Brand Colors, Typography, Logo Usage Guidelines.

## Provenance and verification

- Original Logo geometry and typography were extracted as vector paths from the nine-page Affinity PDF source. No Logo shapes were redrawn or AI-generated.
- New public exports use approved brand black `#000000`, correcting previous near-black `#231F20` in some PDF wordmarks. Affinity source should be checked next time it is edited.
- Reverse-white `08` export omits the black artboard preview background and has transparency for deep-colour placement.
- PNGs are 1000px wide except the horizontal version (1600px wide).
- The editable Affinity `.af` remains the authoritative master for design work; confirm final professional print proofs with suppliers.

## Historical assets

Files previously in `logos/current/` or `logos/source/` are preserved. Historical physical materials do not require replacement. Future social, print, clothing and website application templates will be released separately as Brand Applications Manual assets.
